# Value Equation — framework + scoring rubric

Hormozi's Value Equation ($100M Offers):

```
        Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
        Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

Value rises when you INCREASE the numerator or DECREASE the denominator. Most weak offers only tweak price — price is not in the equation; it's what value is exchanged against.

## The four variables

| Variable | What it means | Levers |
|----------|--------------|--------|
| **Dream Outcome** | The status/state the buyer really wants (not the feature) | Name the end state, make it identity-level ("be the founder who…"), specific and vivid |
| **Perceived Likelihood** | Buyer's belief IT WILL WORK FOR THEM | Proof, guarantees, risk reversal, "people like me" case studies, mechanism explanation |
| **Time Delay** | Time from payment → felt result | Fast first win ("result in 48h"), milestone schedule, done-for-you starts |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** | What buyer must give up / do | Remove steps, templates, done-with-you, no lifestyle change required |

## Scoring rubric (score each offer 1–10 per variable)

**Dream Outcome**
- 1–3: feature-speak ("unlimited matches", "AI-powered")
- 4–6: benefit-speak ("meet people faster")
- 7–8: outcome with specifics ("3 real conversations with people in your exact situation this week")
- 9–10: identity + time-bound + vivid ("stop being the person who's alone in this — first real ally within 7 days")

**Perceived Likelihood**
- 1–3: bare claim, no proof
- 4–6: generic social proof ("1000+ users")
- 7–8: mechanism + relevant case study + weak guarantee
- 9–10: strong risk reversal ("match or free"), named case studies matching buyer's situation

**Time Delay** (lower delay = higher score)
- 1–3: result vague/months away
- 4–6: result in weeks, no first-win
- 7–8: first win < 1 week, explicit
- 9–10: immediate first win at signup + fast full result, stated in the offer

**Effort & Sacrifice** (lower effort = higher score)
- 1–3: buyer does everything (long onboarding, daily grind)
- 4–6: some setup, some ongoing work
- 7–8: minutes to start, system does heavy lifting
- 9–10: near-zero effort, done-for-you or fully automatic, stated in the offer

**Composite = DO + PL + TD + ES (max 40).** Rank all 10 offers in a table. Rewrite top 3.

## Offer structure (each of the 10 offers)

1. **Name** — outcome-loaded, not clever ("The 48-Hour First-Ally Guarantee")
2. **Core promise** — dream outcome + timeframe
3. **Mechanism** — one line: why this works when alternatives don't
4. **Stack** — core + 2–4 bonuses, each attacking one equation variable (bonus that cuts effort, bonus that adds proof…)
5. **Guarantee / risk reversal** — conditional or unconditional, tied to Perceived Likelihood
6. **Price framing** — anchor vs cost of problem, not vs competitors
7. **Scarcity/urgency** — only if REAL (cohort size, founding price); never fabricate

## Rewriting top 3 to irresistible

For each: raise its two LOWEST-scoring variables specifically. Show before/after score. An offer scoring 9-9-3-4 gets a fast-win milestone and effort removal, not more proof.
