# Proof taxonomy + proof-story structure

Proof answers ONE buyer question: "will this work for someone like me?" Different proof types answer it differently — collect across all four, flag empty categories honestly.

## The 4 categories

| Category | What it is | Examples | Strength |
|----------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Quantitative** | Numbers, aggregates | users, matches made, retention %, time-to-result, revenue | High if specific; weak if round/vague |
| **Qualitative** | Voice of user | testimonials, DMs, reviews, screenshots | High when specific + situational ("as a single dad in Oslo…") |
| **Transformation** | Before → after journey | case study with timeline, starting state, obstacle, result | Highest — carries pain, mechanism, outcome |
| **Authority** | Borrowed credibility | press, expert endorsement, credentials, research alignment, awards | Amplifies others; weak alone |

## Gathering rules

- Sources: app reviews, support emails, DMs, community posts, analytics, user interviews. Research protocol from SKILL.md for public sources.
- Every proof asset needs: source, date, permission status (can we publish? anonymize?).
- Empty category = REPORT IT as a gap + suggest cheapest way to fill (e.g., "email 10 active users one question"). NEVER invent proof — fabricated testimonials are illegal in most markets (FTC, EU UCPD) and destroy trust.

## 3-length rewrite (every asset)

- **Short (1 line):** the single most concrete detail. "19 days from moving to Berlin to a weekly crew."
- **Mid (3–4 lines):** situation → what they did → result. Fits a carousel slide or reply.
- **Long (full story):** the proof-story structure below. Fits post/email/landing section.

## Proof-story structure (logic + emotion)

1. **Hook** — the before-state at its worst moment (use a PAIN formula)
2. **Struggle** — what they tried that failed (validates the reader's failed attempts)
3. **Turn** — discovery of the mechanism (product enters HERE, not earlier)
4. **Result** — specific, time-bound, measurable (quantitative proof embedded)
5. **Reader bridge** — "if you're also [situation]…" one-line CTA

20 proof-stories target: mix across categories, weight toward transformation. Reuse strongest assets in multiple stories with different hooks.
