# Hook formulas — 4 families + scoring rubric

A hook is the first 1–2 seconds / first line. Job: stop the scroll, earn the next line. 10–15 words. One idea per hook.

## Family 1 — PAIN (agitate what hurts)

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Call out the moment | "You downloaded 4 dating apps and still eat dinner alone." |
| Name the silent fear | "Nobody tells you how lonely a new city gets after week two." |
| Cost of inaction | "Every month you wait, the people like you pair off without you." |
| "Still doing X?" | "Still swiping on people who can't spell your situation?" |
| Us vs the broken default | "Dating apps match faces. Your problem was never faces." |

## Family 2 — DESIRE (paint the dream)

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Imagine/picture | "Imagine your first coffee with someone who's living your exact chapter." |
| After-state snapshot | "Three weeks in: group chat, Sunday hikes, zero small talk." |
| Permission | "You're allowed to skip strangers and go straight to your people." |
| Specific fantasy number | "One match that gets it beats 200 that don't." |
| Identity claim | "Be the person whose calendar filled up the month they moved." |

## Family 3 — PROOF (lead with evidence)

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Result up front | "847 new-parents matched in 30 days. Here's the mechanism." |
| Unlikely-winner story | "She'd deleted every app. One context match later —" |
| Before/after delta | "From 0 friends in Berlin to a weekly crew in 19 days." |
| Borrowed authority | "The matching logic a Stanford friendship study points at." |
| Receipts tease | "I screenshotted every match that turned into a real friendship." |

## Family 4 — CURIOSITY (open a loop)

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Counterintuitive claim | "Shared interests are the worst predictor of real connection." |
| Secret/mistake | "The one filter that kills 90% of good matches." |
| Question they can't answer | "Why do you click instantly with some strangers?" |
| Withhold the noun | "This is the thing dating apps optimized away." |
| Numbered promise | "3 signals someone will actually show up — most miss #2." |

## Scoring rubric (score every hook 1–5 on each; total /15)

- **Clarity** — 5: instantly understood by a stranger; 1: needs context
- **Curiosity** — 5: unbearable open loop; 1: complete statement, no reason to continue
- **Pull** — 5: target person feels "this is about ME"; 1: generic anyone-bait

Rank all hooks; keep top 25. Rewrite top 10 in 3 formats: short-video opener (spoken, punchy), text-post first line, ad headline (≤8 words).

## Rules

- 10–15 words. Cut every word that doesn't add stop-power.
- Concrete beats clever: numbers, moments, named situations.
- Never fabricate stats/results in PROOF hooks — pull real ones from context.md/proof.md, or mark placeholder `[REAL STAT NEEDED]`.
